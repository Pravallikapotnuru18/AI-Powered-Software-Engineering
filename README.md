The Future of Software Engineering: The Rise of AI-Powered Development

Overview

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way software is designed, developed, tested, and maintained. AI-powered development is helping software engineers automate repetitive tasks, improve code quality, accelerate development workflows, and build intelligent applications.

This article explores the role of Artificial Intelligence in modern software engineering, its emerging technologies, practical applications, challenges, and future opportunities.

Objectives

- Understand the impact of AI on software engineering.
- Explore AI-powered coding and development tools.
- Examine automated software testing and debugging.
- Understand the role of Generative AI and AI agents.
- Identify challenges related to security, reliability, and responsible AI adoption.
